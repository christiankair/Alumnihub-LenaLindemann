QUESTIONS

Good to know

Who can join?

Former ERGO employees who left on good terms are welcome to join the newsletter. Return Tickets are given separately, based on documented criteria.

Is returning guaranteed?

No. Joining the ERGO Lounge or holding a Return Ticket is an invitation to talk, not a promise of a job. Every application follows our standard hiring process.

How is my data used?

We store your contact details and preferences to send what you chose. We do not add performance information to your alumni record. You can update, export or delete your data, or unsubscribe, at any time.

How do I unsubscribe?

Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. You can also contact Christian Kellner and we will remove you.

Questions? Talk to a person.
